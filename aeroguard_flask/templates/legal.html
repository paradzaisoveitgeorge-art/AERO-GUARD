Clause 12 · Disclaimer of Agency ADM Liability & Software Limitations

The Provider assumes zero financial or legal liability for any fines, lost revenue, or ticketing disputes resulting from system warnings, missed notifications, or software errors.
  • No Airline Representation. AERO-GUARD is an independent technology platform and does not speak for, act on behalf of, or represent any commercial airline or GDS consolidator.

Clause 3 · Seat Allocation, Whitelisting, and Account Security
  • Strict Seat Caps. The Customer (Travel Agency) is granted a non-transferable right to access the Software up to the pre-approved maximum "Seat Count" specified in their active subscription tier.

C · Data Privacy & Tenancy Isolation Policy
Clause 5 · Data Security & Multi-Tenant Isolation

The Provider implements robust logical data segregation to ensure that Passenger Name Records (PNRs), fare calculations, financial metrics, and accounting export data belonging to the Customer remain strictly isolated and inaccessible to any competing third-party travel agency utilising the AERO-GUARD platform. All data transmissions are encrypted in transit and at rest.
